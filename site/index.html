The Product

Why Spanish Jamón
Is a Category of Its Own

Most buyers encounter jamón ibérico once and never look back. Here is why this product stands apart from everything else on the charcuterie board.

La Dehesa Ecosystem

Iberian pigs roam free across ancient oak-forested pastures called dehesa — in the montanera season they fatten exclusively on bellotas.

Minimum 2–4 Years of Curing

Top-grade jamón ibérico de bellota is cured for a minimum of 36–48 months in mountain-air bodegas.

A Transparent Label System

Spain's official labelling law defines four quality tiers by breed purity and diet. Every product we supply carries the correct label — no guesswork.

Protected Designations of Origin

Four DOPs legally guarantee regional origin and production method. These are legal certifications backed by EU law — not marketing language.

DOP Jabugo DOP Guijuelo DOP Dehesa de Extremadura DOP Los Pedroches
